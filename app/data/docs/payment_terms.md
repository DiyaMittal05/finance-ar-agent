# Standard Payment Terms (Northstar Supply Co. — synthetic)

All content in this document is synthetic and exists for testing the AR assistant.

## Default terms
Unless a customer contract says otherwise, invoices are payable **Net 30**: payment is due 30 days after the invoice issue date.

Some customers are on **Net 45** or **Net 60** terms. The customer's terms are stored on the customer record in the ERP and in their contract. When the two disagree, the signed contract wins.

## Early payment discount
Customers on **2/10 Net 30** terms may deduct 2% from the invoice amount if they pay within 10 days of the issue date. The full amount is otherwise due in 30 days. The discount cannot be taken after the 10-day window.

## Late fees
A late fee of **1.5% per month** applies to overdue balances after a **5-day grace period**, unless the customer contract waives or changes it. Late fees are never applied to an invoice that is formally disputed while the dispute is open.

Late fees must be stated as a percentage per month. Staff and automated systems must not invent flat penalties or rates that are not in this document or the customer contract.

## Partial payments
Partial payments are applied to the oldest open invoice first, unless the customer specifies an invoice number. The remaining balance stays on the original due date.

## Payment methods
We accept ACH and wire transfer at no cost. Card payments carry a 3% processing surcharge.
